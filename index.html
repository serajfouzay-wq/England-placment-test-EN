Is the English level test free?

Yes. It gives an estimated CEFR starting level and a suggested next step. A short conversation with a teacher can confirm your level.

How long does the test take?

Most students complete it in around 10–12 minutes. Please complete it independently for the most useful recommendation.

Which English courses are available?

Courses include General English, Workplace English, and IELTS preparation. An adviser can help match your course to your goals.

Can I book a consultation first?

Yes. A no-pressure consultation lets you discuss your level, goals, and preferred schedule before deciding on a course.
